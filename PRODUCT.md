# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages from the `rehancodes-web/portfolio` repo.

## Users

Primary: founders and small-business owners deciding whether to hire Rehan to build a website, web app, automation or AI tool. They arrive from LinkedIn or a shared link, often on a phone, and judge within seconds whether he can make something that would impress their own customers. Secondary: recruiters and hackathon or startup peers.

## Product Purpose

Rehan Nadaf's personal portfolio. It must prove design and engineering skill through the site itself, show the products he has built, and turn a visiting business owner into a build request.

## Positioning

A student founder who ships real products: Co-Founder & CTO at Executive Board (an AI executive board and ERP for founders, live at executiveboard.in) and CTO at Vergemount (PLAYYR). The site is itself the demonstration: interactive, hand-built, voiced.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse projects, hear or read each project's story, then contact Rehan by email (mailto form), LinkedIn or GitHub. Contact email: rehanzn2007@gmail.com (user-approved for public display).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Projects (all real): Executive Board, PLAYYR, EatOnGo, HigherUp.AI, put1meet, Community Manager (web + iOS), Campus Paws, Polymath OS, and this portfolio.
- Each project has a pre-recorded narration in an Indian English voice (Microsoft Prabhat neural, via edge-tts; regenerate with `py tools/revoice_edge.py`) in `audio/`, with sentence timings in `audio/narration.json`.
- Services offered: websites, web apps and dashboards, automations, AI assistants and integrations, mobile apps, tech partner for founders. Quote only, no prices.
- No server: the contact form opens the visitor's email app.

## Brand Commitments

- The user asked for a design that impresses businesses through design skill; no existing look is binding. The user rejected the previous purple palette as looking AI-generated.
- Voice: plain, confident, first person from Rehan; no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- Awards: 1st place AIESEC Ideathon (EatOnGo); 4th place Hack4SDG by AIESEC in Bengaluru (EatOnGo, team Code Larpers); Top 5 SAP Hackathon; Top 5 SAP Ideathon.
- Live links: executiveboard.in, rehancodes-web.github.io/eatongo/, rehancodes-web.github.io/HigherUp.AI/, rehancodes-web.github.io/put1meet/put1meet/, rehancodes-web.github.io/cie3webtech/.
- No client testimonials, client logos, user metrics or prices exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The site is the proof: every interaction should show craft a client could buy.
2. Real work only: no invented clients, numbers or testimonials.
3. One clear next step: request a build.
4. Fast and usable on a phone first.
